# === Answer ===
# The requirements for housing a raptor include:

# - Adequate facilities must be ready to house raptors, with proper weighing scales, leashes, swivels, and bath containers available (falconry id law.pdf, p.2).
# - Facilities must provide shelter from wind, rain, snow, and sun, with at least two corners covered to protect each raptor (Raptor-Housing-Information.pdf, p.1).
# - Raptors should have access to water for drinking and bathing, and facilities must be kept clean and free of odors, parasites, and disease (Raptor-Housing-Information.pdf, p.1).
# - Raptors should have perches and equipment in good, working order (Raptor-Housing-Information.pdf, p.1).
# - Raptors should be protected from domestic animals and excessive exposure to weather conditions (Raptor-Housing-Information.pdf, p.1).

# Facility inspections are conducted by state officials or their designees, who may inspect facilities without advance notice during business hours (falconry id law.pdf, p.2, facilities-handbook MN.pdf, p.2).
